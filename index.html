Healthy Living Tips

Small, consistent changes make the biggest difference.

🥗

Nutrition

Fill half your plate with vegetables, choose whole grains, and limit sugary drinks and ultra-processed food.

🏃

Exercise

Aim for 150 minutes of moderate activity per week plus 2 days of strength training.

🌙

Sleep

Keep a consistent schedule, avoid screens an hour before bed, and keep your room cool and dark.

🧠

Mental Health

Take breaks, practise deep breathing, stay connected with people, and ask for help when you need it.

💧

Hydration

Drink water regularly through the day — more when it's hot or when you exercise.

🚭

Avoid Harmful Habits

Avoid smoking, limit alcohol, and get regular health check-ups.
